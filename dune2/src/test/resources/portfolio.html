Research

Newborough Warren: groundwater in a coastal sand dune aquifer

A long-term study of the water table beneath the Newborough Warren dunes on Anglesey, a Special Area of Conservation. Monthly dipwell readings and public climate data run through a reproducible analysis pipeline that sorts the wells into behavioural groups, tests forestry and habitat interventions, and supports operational flood forecasting.

Main findingThe Corsican pine plantation both uses water and shades the water table from evaporation. Clearfelling removes both effects, so the water table is expected to fall rather than recover.
